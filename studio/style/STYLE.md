# Study Buddy — style des images de questions

Le « graphiste » (Nano Banana Pro, voir le DESIGNER-PLAYBOOK de Spotlight) dessine les images
des questions de Ryan (3e année). Les pages de ses cahiers servent de RÉFÉRENCE DE STYLE
seulement: on crée des exercices originaux, on ne recopie jamais une page du cahier.

## Prompt block (prepend to every generation)

A clean illustration for a Québec grade 3 (8-year-old) school workbook exercise, in the same
friendly, colourful, flat style as the attached workbook page photos. Pure white background,
crisp vector-like shapes, soft pastel colours, thin dark outlines, lots of white space, nothing
cluttered. Friendly, warm, never scary. Evenly lit, no shadows on the background, no paper
texture, no photo of a book, no hands, no pencil, no page edges: output only the illustration
itself, as if it were printed on the page.

## Rules

- NO TEXT in the image unless the brief explicitly asks for a number or a word. The question,
  the choices and the answer live in the app, never in the picture (the model misspells).
- COUNTS MUST BE EXACT. When the brief says "22 rods", there are 22 rods. Group items so they
  are easy to count (rows of 5 or 10, clear gaps). Every math image is counted by Claude before
  it enters the app; a wrong count = regenerate or drop, never "close enough".
- Base-10 blocks (Matcha style): gros cube = unité de mille (a 10×10×10 cube), plaque = centaine
  (a flat 10×10 square), bâtonnet = dizaine (a 1×10 rod), petit cube = unité (a small single cube).
  Same colour family as the workbook (light blue / beige wood tones), drawn in 3/4 view.
- People and animals: original characters only (Gaston le cochon d'Inde, Mademoiselle Rose
  l'entraîneuse, Capitaine Barbe-Grise, Léo, Maya). Never characters from the books.
- Diversity and kindness: kids of different backgrounds, no mocking a body type.
- Show ONE sample before any batch. The parent sees every image before Ryan does.
