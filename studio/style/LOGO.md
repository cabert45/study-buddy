# Study Buddy — logo (marque / icône d'app)

L'app sert des enfants de 5 à 13 ans (maternelle → secondaire 1) et c'est ce que les parents voient
en premier. Le logo doit donner envie: chaleureux et amical, mais propre et « vrai produit », pas
un dessin de cahier. Couleurs de l'app choisies par Cayla: orange.

## Prompt block (prepend to every generation)

A professional app logo mark for "Study Buddy", a friendly study app for kids and pre-teens.
Flat vector logo design, like a polished iOS app icon symbol: one simple, bold, memorable shape,
geometric and balanced, perfectly centered on a pure white background with generous empty
margins. Warm brand palette only: burnt orange #C74A15, bright orange #E8622A, a touch of warm
amber #F59E0B, soft cream #FEFAF6, and dark brown #2C2017 for tiny details. Smooth clean
curves, crisp edges, at most one subtle gradient, no photorealism, no 3D render, no texture,
no drop shadow on the background, no mockup, no hands, no frame or border around the canvas.
It must stay recognizable when shrunk to a 32-pixel favicon.

## Rules

- NO TEXT, no letters, no words in the image — the "Study Buddy" wordmark is set in code (Baloo 2).
- One mark only, not a sheet of variations.
- Friendly, never childish-scary; works for a 5-year-old and a 12-year-old.
- Original design only: no existing brand, character or mascot (no Duolingo owl, no Sanrio, etc.).
- The parent picks the concept; nothing ships before approval.
